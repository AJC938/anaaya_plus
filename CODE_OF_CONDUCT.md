# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and reviewers are expected to communicate professionally, respectfully, and constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give technical feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its engineering goals.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete problems clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, or publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.
